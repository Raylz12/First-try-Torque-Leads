Got Questions

COMMON QUESTIONS.

Do you work with any type of auto shop?
Our system is built specifically around heavy-duty diesel Class 6–8 trucks, fleet maintenance, commercial diesel. That's where we get results. If you're outside that niche, we could discuss whether our approach makes sense for your situation. Reach out and let's talk.
How fast do we see results?
Most shops start seeing qualified lead flow within 2–4 weeks of launch. Social media content builds authority immediately. Tech recruiting campaigns typically produce applications within the first month.
Are the leads exclusive to my shop?
Yes 100% exclusive. We only take one client per MSA (Metropolitan Statistical Area). If your market is open, you lock it out for every competitor in your metro. We don't resell leads, share data, or work with more than one shop in your area. Ever.
What's an MSA and do you serve my area?
An MSA (Metropolitan Statistical Area) is the metro region around your city defined by the U.S. Census Bureau. We work with diesel shops in any MSA across the country. Big metros, mid-size markets, rural corridors if there are fleets in your area, we can run campaigns there. Book a free 15-minute call and we'll tell you whether your market is currently open.
What does it cost?
Pricing is custom based on your market size, competition, and goals. We don't post rates publicly book a 15-minute call and we'll give you a straight answer based on what we'd actually do for your specific shop.
Do I have to sign a long-term contract?
No long-term contracts. We earn your business month over month. If you're not seeing results, you're not locked in.
